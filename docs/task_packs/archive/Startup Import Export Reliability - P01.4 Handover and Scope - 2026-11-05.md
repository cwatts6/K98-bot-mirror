## Non-negotiable learning carried forward

1. Keep four axes distinct: business commit/result, physical worker state, diagnostic/audit availability, notification delivery. Observation timeout or late completion is not business failure. Preserve the original result/exception and ownership until physical settlement; releasing a reservation is not completing the intended work.
2. Diagnostics must not prevent entry, acquire a reader lock, introduce blocking dependencies/contention, extend processing critical sections, trigger repair/retry or replace a committed result. Optional logging is optional; immutable business custody is not. Establish acceptance and budgets before implementing and retain adverse results.
3. Display/report reads remain passive even when unknown, stale, busy or after restart. Five-minute existing dashboard rereads and manual Refresh are sufficient; no extra observation worker. Unknown values never become healthy/zero by default. Retained observations do not authorize writes.
4. P01.3 decisions remain settled: 60-minute provider freshness; separate recovery 90-second completed-cycle freshness and 300/900 progress rules; actionable alerts through existing status/recovery routes; no automatic log scan, last-20-errors or count-spike alerts; explicit `/ops logs` retained. Whole-bot outage monitoring was deliberately not added. Preserve command/card agreement and accepted persistent Refresh behavior.
5. Persistent UI callbacks acknowledge early, bind actor/context/identity and survive restart through bounded registration. Drain owned edits on shutdown/replacement. Confirmed refusal/deletion differs from uncertain send; do not duplicate on uncertainty or promise memory-only durability.
6. Test the final delivered outcome and actual caller boundaries. P01.3's collector passed while the ZIP silently omitted a member. Compare all available components to final archive members, hashes, sizes and bounded continuation; protect these regression tests when changing evidence contracts. Apply the same end-to-end principle to import summaries, queued audit and callback/ack sequencing.
7. Honest report metadata includes source, capture times, age, partial coverage and continuation. Generic unknown descriptor fields are not lost source-specific health timestamps. Preserve actor binding, redaction, frozen retry, budgets and incident priority. Reports/logs remain evidence, not proof of provider liveness or business completion.
8. Avoid self-created failures. P01.2b proactive startup instrumentation was rejected: an observer changed child exit 37 to 120, and its replacement measured 3443.3 ms cold attach against 500 ms, setup p95 about 496 ms against 50 ms. It is closed by decision, not an outstanding feature. Use existing logs/receipts and small incident-driven improvements; do not resurrect the observer.
9. Measure the actual boundaries without telemetry amplification. P01.3 retained failed benchmark runs, corrected a simulator early-wake artefact and met unchanged limits. No green focused suite substitutes for affected-caller/concurrency/restart review or P05 final confirmation.
10. Protect release authority: separate immutable mirror/private reviewed heads, exact final diffs and checks, actual installed updater predecessor evidence, additive successors and unchanged historical pins. Deploy only private `K98-bot/main`; no blind downgrade, broad bypass or target-only updater permission. Read-only closeout does not require a production restart.
